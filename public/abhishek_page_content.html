Executive Biography & Professional Profile

Abhishek Kumar Chaurasiya is a dedicated Structural Engineer and public-sector engineering professional specializing in earthquake engineering, advanced structural mechanics, resilient infrastructure planning, and sustainable urban development. He currently serves as an Engineer with Nepal's Ministry of Infrastructure Development, contributing to strategic planning, safety evaluations, design standards, and implementation of high-resilience public buildings and civic infrastructure.

He completed his Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Civil Engineering from M. S. Ramaiah Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, graduating with First Class with Distinction and earning several of the institute's highest academic honors, including the Gold Medal, Best Student Award, Best Project Award, and Best Achiever Award. He subsequently completed his Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Structural Engineering at the world-renowned Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bombay, where his research centered on structural dynamics, nonlinear computational modeling, and performance-based earthquake engineering.

His professional and research endeavors emphasize earthquake-resistant design, seismic retrofitting of existing lifeline structures, base isolation systems, structural health monitoring (SHM), and sustainable building technologies tailored to the challenging seismic landscape of the Himalayan region. Through evidence-based engineering, academic publications, and interdisciplinary collaboration, he works to bridge the gap between academic research and modern public infrastructure execution.

कार्यकारी जीवनी (नेपालीमा)

इ. अभिषेक कुमार चौरसिया भूकम्प इन्जिनियरिङ, संरचनात्मक विश्लेषण, विपद् लचिलो पूर्वाधार, र दिगो शहरी विकासमा विशेषज्ञता हासिल गरेका एक संरचनात्मक इन्जिनियर (Structural Engineer) र सार्वजनिक क्षेत्रका पेशेवर हुन्। उनी हाल नेपाल सरकारको पूर्वाधार विकास मन्त्रालयमा इन्जिनियरको रूपमा कार्यरत रही नेपालको निर्मित वातावरणको सुरक्षा, स्थायित्व र दिगोपन बढाउने पूर्वाधार आयोजनाहरूको योजना, डिजाइन र प्राविधिक कार्यान्वयनमा योगदान पुर्‍याउँदै आउनुभएको छ।

उहाँले बेंगलुरुस्थित एम. एस. रमैया इन्स्टिच्युट अफ टेक्नोलोजीबाट सिभिल इन्जिनियरिङमा स्नातक (B.E.) विशिष्ट श्रेणीमा पूरा गर्नुभयो र स्वर्ण पदक (Gold Medal), उत्कृष्ट विद्यार्थी पुरस्कार, उत्कृष्ट परियोजना पुरस्कार र उत्कृष्ट उपलब्धि पुरस्कार प्राप्त गर्नुभयो। तत्पश्चात, उहाँले प्रतिष्ठित भारतीय प्रविधि संस्थान (IIT) बम्बईबाट संरचनात्मक इन्जिनियरिङमा मास्टर अफ टेक्नोलोजी (M.Tech) पूरा गर्नुभयो।

इन्जिनियरिङ अभ्यासको अलावा, उहाँ चौरसिया समाज नेपालको कार्यकारी नेतृत्वमा रही समुदायका स्रोतहरूको डिजिटलाइजेसन, युवा सशक्तीकरण र प्राविधिक विकासमा सक्रिय भूमिका निर्वाह गर्दै आउनुभएको छ।
